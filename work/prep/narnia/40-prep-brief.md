## Meaning to preserve

> A Christian redemption arc beneath a fairy-tale adventure: Aslan's voluntary, substitutionary
> sacrifice in the traitor Edmund's place, his resurrection on the third dawn by a Deeper Magic older
> than the enemy's knowledge, and his triumphant defeat of evil — the Passion, Atonement, and
> Resurrection carried by **events**, and (per the allegory-as-reception stance) widely received as an
> open allegory of the Christian gospel. Intertwined: the betrayal-forgiveness / coming-of-age theme
> (Edmund's fall and restoration), truthful belief (Lucy) over cynical disbelief, and childlike wonder.
> Lewis's "supposal" caveat is noted authorially, but the allegorical reading dominates reception and
> the structure carries it. **At T3+ the resonance (Aslan=Christ; Stone Table=Passion/Resurrection;
> Deep/Deeper Magic=Law/Gospel) is named openly; at T1–T2 it stays implicit. No tier strips the
> sacrificial-redemption structure, the Deep/Deeper Magic, or the betrayal-forgiveness arc.**
