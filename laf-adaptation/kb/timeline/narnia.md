# Narnia Timeline

## Chapter 01 — Lucy's first crossing into Narnia

- Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy are evacuated from London during the wartime air-raids to the old Professor's large country house, where Mrs. Macready is the housekeeper. [CERTAIN]
- On a rainy day, the children explore indoors and reach an empty room containing one big wardrobe with a looking-glass door. [CERTAIN]
- Lucy stays behind, enters the wardrobe among the fur coats, and crosses into a snowy night wood. [CERTAIN]
- Lucy keeps the wardrobe doorway in sight and walks toward a distant light, which is a lamp-post. [CERTAIN]
- At the lamp-post, Lucy meets the Faun, who is startled and drops his parcels. The Faun is unnamed in Chapter 01. [CERTAIN]

## Chapter 02 — Tea with Mr. Tumnus; the errand and the repentance

> Tier-neutral, SOURCE NAMES ONLY ("Mr. Tumnus", "the White Witch").

- The Faun is named Mr. Tumnus; he confirms Lucy is a Human and tells her she is in the land of Narnia. [CERTAIN]
- Mr. Tumnus takes Lucy to his firelit cave for a splendid tea, tells her tales of forest life, and plays a bewitching straw flute; hours pass. [CERTAIN]
- When Lucy rouses to go home, Mr. Tumnus breaks down and confesses he is in the pay of the White Witch, who keeps Narnia in eternal winter ("always winter and never Christmas"). [CERTAIN]
- Mr. Tumnus confesses he was ordered to kidnap any Human he met and hand them to the Witch; Lucy is the first, and he had meant to lull her asleep and betray her. [CERTAIN]
- Having come to know Lucy as a friend, Mr. Tumnus repents, refuses to give her up despite the Witch's threatened punishments (including being turned to stone until the four thrones at Cair Paravel are filled), and leads her quietly back through the spy-filled wood to the lamp-post. [CERTAIN]
- Lucy forgives Mr. Tumnus, lets him keep her handkerchief, and runs back through the wardrobe into the same empty room, calling out that she has returned safe. [CERTAIN]
