# The Siege at the Grey City (Tier 3, ages 9-11 — transition tier)

Dawn did not come to the plain that morning. Sauron's shadow lay over the world, and his great army marched on the Grey City in a line of iron. The dark lord did not ride with them. Yet every soldier felt his cold will pressing down from the black tower in the east.

On the walls, the Steward Denethor watched the enemy come, and something in him had already given way. He had looked too long into the darkness, and the darkness had looked back. He whispered that all was ended, and he would not answer when the guards called to him. He turned his face to the wall while the city needed him most.

Then the horns of the mountain-riders rang out. Old King Théoden led his men down against the army. The fighting was hard and the stakes were real. Then one of the Nazgûl came. These were dark riders whose cry drained the courage from brave men. The rider struck the king down, and he fell. Théoden died there on the field.

But Éowyn would not run. She stood over her fallen king and faced the rider alone. She fought it and struck it down, and its shadow screamed and was gone. Many soldiers had died and would not rise again, and the field was heavy with grief. Yet the city held, and the iron army broke against it and drew back toward the east. When the sun finally rose above the smoke, the Grey City was still standing. It had been bought at a bitter price, but it stood.
