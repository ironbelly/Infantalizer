# The Big Tidy-Up at the Grey City (Tier 1, draft v2 — revised per editorial memo)

The sky was grey and sleepy that morning. Far away lived the Grumpy King. His land had no sunshine at all. That made him feel very grumpy. His noisy helpers marched to the Grey City. They wanted to make a big mess.

High on the walls stood the Sad Leader. He had closed all of his windows. So his room felt dark and gloomy. But dark rooms can always be opened again. He turned away and would not talk yet.

Then the mountain friends heard the horns. Brave Théoden led them out to help. Together they would tidy the big mess. The Grumpy Riders rumbled and grumbled loudly. One bumped the old king by mistake. He grew very tired and sat right down.

But kind Éowyn did not run away. She stood beside the tired old king. She spoke gently and kindly to the Grumpy Rider. Her kind words helped the grumpy shadow feel calm. So the shy shadow smiled and hurried home. Many helpers were tired and needed a rest.

Soon the noisy grumbles hurried away east. The friends tidied the whole big mess. The Grey City felt safe and happy. Then the warm sun came out to play. It found the Grey City still standing. Someone opened the Sad Leader's windows wide. The sunshine made his gloomy room bright again. Everyone shared a hug and a little rest.
