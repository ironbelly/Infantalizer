## Safety-rope visibility (T1-specific carry)

This is the T1 safety-rope — the youngest reader can always see the way back. It is a rendering emphasis (the source keeps the doorway in sight but does not spell out the reassurance); logged here so later chapters that strand Lucy further (e.g. Mr. Tumnus's cave) are flagged at T1 continuity for the same safety-rope discipline.
