## ch-01 — Lucy Finds the Snowy Land

*After ch-01, a Tier-1 reader knows:*

### Setting / world
- There were four children: **Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy**.
- They **went to live far away in a big old house** (war/evacuation NOT known to the T1 reader — rendered as a neutral move).
- The house belongs to **a kind old man, the Professor**, with wild white hair.
- **Mrs. Macready** keeps the house tidy; she gave Lucy a biscuit and a smile. Lucy felt safe right away.
- The house has many rooms to explore: a room full of pictures, a shiny suit of armour, and one small empty room containing **one big cupboard** (tall and dark).

### Characters (T1 renderings — NOT source canon)
- **Peter** — leads the exploration ("Let us explore!").
- **Susan** — present; one of the four.
- **Edmund** — was **grumpy and tired that first night**, but **felt bright again next day**. (T1: grumpiness is a passing mood, not a trait. No sarcasm/mockery known to the T1 reader.)
- **Lucy** — curious and brave; the one who enters the cupboard.
- **The Professor** — kind old man, wild white hair.
- **Mrs. Macready** — housekeeper; kind to Lucy (biscuit + smile).
- **The Faun** (unnamed at T1) — a little person as tall as Lucy, curly hair under a red scarf, little goat-hoofs, carried an umbrella and brown paper parcels. **He is a FRIEND**: kind face, warm smile, laughed with Lucy, said "Hello, Lucy" and smiled at her. (T1 PROMISE — see `decisions.md`: this friendship cannot be broken at tier-1.)

### Place: the snowy land (through the cupboard)
- The cupboard is a **portal**: soft furry coats inside, smelling of fur and mothballs; then crunch of snow underfoot, rough branches brushing the face, a light far ahead.
- Through the door is **a wood at night** with snow falling. The trees are dark and tall but the snow makes it bright.
- **Lucy could still see the cupboard door** — she could go home. (T1 safety-rope: the retreat path is VISIBLE. Later chapters that strand Lucy must honor this discipline or flag the break.)
- A **lamp-post** stands in the middle of the wood, warm and soft. Lucy finds it funny — a lamp-post in a wood.

### Plot state
- Lucy has **crossed into the snowy land** and **met the Faun**.
- The encounter ended **warmly**: they laughed, exchanged names, the Faun smiled. (No cliffhanger, no threat, no unresolved tension for the T1 reader.)
- The other three children (Peter, Susan, Edmund) do **not** know where Lucy is — they left the empty room believing "Nothing here!"

## ch-02 — Tea with the Kind Faun

*After ch-02, a Tier-1 reader now also knows:*

### Names now introduced (were withheld at ch-01)
- The kind Faun is named **Mr. Tumnus**.
- The snowy land is named **Narnia**.

### Characters (T1 renderings — NOT source canon)
- **Mr. Tumnus** — the same kind Faun from ch-01. He has a cosy little cave, gives Lucy a lovely tea, tells fun stories, and plays a sweet flute. He is Lucy's **friend**. (T1 FRIEND-PROMISE from ch-01 D3 remains **honored** — he is never a threat.)
- **The Grumpy Witch** (off-page, never seen) — a grumpy character who "makes it snow and snow" and "never lets it be Christmas." She has a helper-in-the-wood, but she never appears or does anything on-page. (External-state archetype; NOT called evil.)
- **Mr. Tumnus's father** — a good old Faun in a picture on the cave wall.

### Place
- **Mr. Tumnus's cave** — a warm, cosy home (fire, rug, two chairs, tea). A safe place.
- The **lamp-post** is the turning-back point: from there Lucy can see "the cupboard door … the way home."

### Plot state
- Lucy had **tea with Mr. Tumnus** and heard his stories.
- Mr. Tumnus was sad about a **worry**: the Grumpy Witch gave him a **rule** — to bring her any girl he met. He **could never do that to a friend**, so he **did not**.
- Lucy comforted him; he cheered up; he **walked Lucy safely home** to the lamp-post.
- Lucy **got home** through the cupboard and called out that she is "all right." (No open threat, no unresolved fear — chapter closes warm.)
- Lucy gave Mr. Tumnus her **handkerchief** to keep.
