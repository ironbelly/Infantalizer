## Source facts

- Four children named Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy are evacuated from London during the wartime air-raids to the old Professor's large country house.
- The Professor owns the large country house; Mrs. Macready is the Professor's housekeeper.
- On the first evening, the children meet the Professor. Lucy is a little afraid of his odd appearance, and Edmund tries not to laugh.
- That night, the children gather in the girls' room and agree the house will be splendid to explore. Edmund is prickly, and an owl calls.
- The next morning is rainy, so the children decide to explore indoors.
- During the indoor exploration, the children pass through a series of rooms, including rooms with pictures and a suit of armour, a green room with a harp, and a balcony.
- The children finally enter an empty room containing one big wardrobe with a looking-glass door.
- Peter says, "Nothing there!" and the others leave the room.
- Lucy stays behind, opens the wardrobe door, and steps in among the fur coats.
- Lucy walks deeper into the wardrobe, expecting to find its wooden back, but instead feels soft powdery cold underfoot and rough prickly branches.
- Lucy finds herself in a snowy wood at night with snowflakes falling.
- Lucy keeps the wardrobe doorway in sight behind her and walks toward a distant light.
- The distant light is a lamp-post standing in the snowy wood.
- At the lamp-post, Lucy hears footsteps and sees a Faun step out from the trees.
- The Faun has goat-legs, hoofs, horns, an umbrella, and brown-paper parcels.
- The Faun is startled on seeing Lucy, drops his parcels, and exclaims, "Goodness gracious me!"
