## Hard source facts (ch-01 — "The Siege at the Grey City")
- **Sauron** (the dark lord) directs an assault on the Grey City from afar; he does not ride with the host.
- **Denethor** (the Steward) has fallen into despair and withdraws from the defense.
- **Théoden** (the old king) leads the mountain-riders against the host and is struck down.
- **Éowyn** stands over the fallen king and destroys the attacking **Nazgûl** rider.
- Many soldiers fall; the city nevertheless holds and the host withdraws eastward.
