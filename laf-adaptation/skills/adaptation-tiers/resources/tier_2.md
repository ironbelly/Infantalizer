# Tier 2 — design commentary (ages 6-8, early concrete operational)

*Rationale only — the authoritative T2 constraint data lives in `kb/tiers/tier_2.yaml`. Carried from
`docs/design_decisions/001-five-tier-system.md` and `003-agency-externalization.md`.*

## Why Tier 2 frames "conflict as contest/competition"

Piaget places ages 6-8 in **early concrete operational** reasoning; Kohlberg is at **stage 2**
(instrumental exchange — fairness, reciprocity, "what's in it for me"). This is where conflict can be
rendered as a **contest or competition** rather than existential threat: a child this age grasps
rules, winning and losing, and fair play, but is not yet equipped for genuine moral ambiguity. Framing
antagonism as a contest keeps the stakes legible without introducing internal malice.

## Agency Externalization is still MANDATORY

Per 003, externalization stays **MANDATORY through Tier 2** because egocentrism persists across ages 3-8
— the same risk of internalizing "badness" applies. Negative outcomes remain a temporary **state** or
**accident**, never an innate quality. Tier 2 differs from Tier 1 not in *whether* it externalizes but in
*how much* narrative complexity the child can now hold around that externalization (contest structure,
simple cause-and-effect chains).

## Why Tier 2 is a distinct tier and not folded into 1 or 3

001 argues explicitly **against collapsing to three tiers**: doing so "misses the critical Tier 2→3
transition" and "forces crude binary choices." Ages 6-8 have distinct cognitive capabilities from both
preschoolers and the age-9-11 transition band, so they earn their own profile. The concrete thresholds
(vocabulary, sentence length, the `contest_paradigm` block) are in `kb/tiers/tier_2.yaml`.
