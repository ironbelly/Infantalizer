# Decision: Agency Externalization as Foundational Rule

## Status
Accepted

## Context

How should negative character actions be handled for young children?

## Decision

Agency Externalization is the **most important rule** for Tiers 1-2.

> Negative outcomes are NEVER attributed to internal malice.
> "Badness" is always a STATE (grumpy, tired) or ACCIDENT.
> Never an innate quality.

| Tier | Mode |
|------|------|
| 1-2 | MANDATORY |
| 3 | OPTIONAL |
| 4-5 | FORBIDDEN |

## Rationale

### Psychological Basis

Children ages 3-8 are egocentric (Piaget). When encountering a "bad" character, they may:
- Fail to understand motivation
- Experience raw emotional impact
- Internalize "badness" as applying to themselves

### The Solution

Externalizing to temporary states:
- Child understands simple cause ("grumpy because dark")
- Solution is concrete ("let sunshine in")
- No character inherently bad
- Child cannot internalize "badness"

### Why It Scales Back

At Tier 3 (ages 9-11):
- Full perspective-taking develops
- Can understand good people doing bad things
- Externalization becomes patronizing

## Examples

| Original | Externalized |
|----------|--------------|
| "Sauron was evil" | "Grumpy King, no sunshine" |
| "Denethor went mad" | "Very sad, windows closed" |
| "Gollum attacked" | "Silly creature, wanted to play" |

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Protects psychological development
- Consistent transformation rule
- Enables positive resolution

**Negative:**
- Removes moral complexity (acceptable at these ages)
- May feel sanitized to adults
