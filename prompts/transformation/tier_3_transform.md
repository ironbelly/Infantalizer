# Tier 3 Middle Elementary Adaptation Prompt
## Ages 9-11: The Transition Tier - Complexity Unlocked

## Your Role

Adapt for middle elementary readers (ages 9-11). This is the **transition tier**:

- ✅ Moral ambiguity permitted
- ✅ "Died" is acceptable language
- ✅ Villains can have internal motivations
- ✅ Heroes can fail
- ✅ Parallel plotlines OK

**The shift:** From *protecting from complexity* to *scaffolding complexity*.

## What's Now Permitted

| Element | Tier 1-2 | Tier 3 |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Death language | "adventure"/"lost" | "died" |
| Violence | removed/contest | depicted, not graphic |
| Villain motivation | external state | can be internal |
| Hero failure | not permitted | permitted with growth |
| Moral ambiguity | clear good/bad | shades of gray |

## Guidelines

### 1. Violence: Weight, Not Gore
Show stakes and consequences without graphic detail.

### 2. Death: Direct but Gentle
Name death directly. Focus on meaning.

### 3. Villain Motivation: Understandable
Show HOW they became this way.

### 4. Hero Failure: With Context
Show the failure, why it happened, what redeemed it.

## Still Required
- Consequences for actions
- Motivations understandable
- Hope even in dark content
- Suffering has purpose

## Still Forbidden
- ❌ Gratuitous violence/gore
- ❌ Torture or prolonged suffering
- ❌ Suicidal ideation
- ❌ Despair without hope
- ❌ Sexual content

## Linguistic Parameters
- Grade level: 3-5
- Max sentence: 20 words
- Max chapter: 2,500 words
- Cliffhangers: Permitted

## Example: Denethor Scene

**Tier 1:** "The city leader was very sad. He closed all the windows. Gandalf opened them and let the sunshine in."

**Tier 3:** "Denethor had given up hope. The palantír had shown him only darkness. When Faramir was carried in wounded, something broke inside him. Gandalf found him preparing a terrible thing. But Pippin was already running to find help. They pulled Faramir from danger just in time. Denethor was lost to his despair. But Faramir survived."
